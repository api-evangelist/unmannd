# Unmannd

> Unmannd (Unmannd Autonomy Pvt Ltd) is an India-based defense technology company engineering autonomous drones — "Engineering Aerial Dominance." It builds autonomous logistics drones and counter-UAS (cUAS) interceptors designed to operate in contested and unpredictable environments. Backed by Accel and Speciale Invest.
